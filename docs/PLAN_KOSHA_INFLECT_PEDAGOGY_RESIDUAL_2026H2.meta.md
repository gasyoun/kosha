## Known limitations / caveats

- The residual inventory was derived from the two roadmaps' **own** text, their
  metadocs, and `roadmap_handoff_truth.py`. Work that exists in kosha but is
  registered on neither roadmap is out of its field of view.
- The K2 census may find the identity gap is smaller than 87 % of the divergence
  implies — the handoff deliberately publishes the census even if it undercuts the
  premise. Do not read this plan as asserting the gap's size.
- N1 and N2 are recorded as human acts. That is a statement about authority, not
  about difficulty; both are small.

## Intended use / known misuse

- **For:** picking the next kosha inflect/pedagogy unit with its gate and its fence
  already stated.
- **Misuse:** reading the "Not mintable" table as a blocker list. Neither N1 nor N2
  blocks anything here — that is precisely why they are listed apart.
