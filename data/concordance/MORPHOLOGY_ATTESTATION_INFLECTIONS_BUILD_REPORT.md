## A¬G triage — lexicon gap vs paradigm gap

An attested form the engine never produced is only a csl-inflect give-back candidate (H185) when the engine **knew the lemma** and still missed the cell. A¬G is therefore split on whether the DCS lemma is in the generator's own lemma inventory (223,269 distinct lemma keys from `inflections.lemma_slp1`: **222,735 nominal** — rows with `person IS NULL` — and **680 verbal**).

| Triage class | Count | share of A¬G | of which `tense_caveat=1` | Meaning |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `lexicon_gap` | 93,228 | 56.76% | 5,296 | DCS lemma absent from the generator's inventory → a dictionary-coverage gap, NOT an inflection-engine bug; never routed to give-back |
| `paradigm_gap` | 70,510 | 42.93% | 5,536 | lemma IS in the generator's inventory, this form never generated → **the genuine engine gap; routes to the csl-inflect give-back (H185)** |
| `non_sanskrit_or_ocr` | 480 | 0.29% | 57 | carries a non-IAST character (digit/Latin/punctuation) → OCR artefact |
| `segmentation_artefact` | 18 | 0.01% | 0 | space-containing or single-character token; DCS segmentation artefact |

**Sandhi control.** 68,049 of the 164,236 A¬G keys (41.43%) are attested **only** as a sandhied surface, never as an unsandhied form — for those the gap is a sandhi-surface artefact of the attested side, not a claim about the generator. The remaining 96,187 are attested in unsandhied form and are the defensible gap set.

**Verb control — read this before quoting `paradigm_gap` as an engine defect.** The generator's inventory is **222,735 nominal lemmas against 680 verbal** (0.30% of it verbal), so finite verbs are territory `inflections` barely claims. Of the 112,368 attested keys DCS tags `upos=VERB`, **28,571 are generated (25.43%)** and **83,797 fall in A¬G**. Per class:

| Triage class | rows | of which DCS `upos=VERB` | share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `lexicon_gap` | 93,228 | 59,659 | 63.99% |
| `paradigm_gap` | 70,510 | 23,942 | 33.96% |
| `non_sanskrit_or_ocr` | 480 | 196 | 40.83% |
| `segmentation_artefact` | 18 | 0 | 0.00% |

A `paradigm_gap` row on a verbal lemma is therefore **not** evidence that the Cologne nominal-inflection tables are broken; it is evidence of the scope they never covered. The give-back candidates worth acting on are the **non-verbal** `paradigm_gap` rows.

**Give-back routing (hard stop).** `paradigm_gap` rows are handed to the csl-inflect dual-engine give-back (H185) as a **queued port** — a GTD row and a pointer, never an in-pass edit to csl-inflect.
