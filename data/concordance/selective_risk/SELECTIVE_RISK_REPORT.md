## Error versus coverage

`strict` counts every `different` verdict; `lenient` drops the `near_miss` ones
(metonymy, POS variant, sibling referent). The truth is bracketed by the pair.
Rates are stratified estimates re-weighted to the population, never raw deck fractions.

| scope | eligible rows | coverage | cards | strict wrong-match | 95 % CI | lenient | 95 % CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---|
| all aligned | 6,860 | 10.20 % | 60 | 20.0 % | 10.0–35.4 % | 10.1 % | 3.0–24.2 % |
| score >= 0.40 | 5,388 | 8.01 % | 40 | 18.4 % | 6.8–34.8 % | 10.2 % | 1.6–26.1 % |
| score >= 0.70 | 2,132 | 3.17 % | 20 | 12.6 % | 0.3–36.0 % | 7.4 % | 0.0–30.1 % |

The point estimate falls monotonically with confidence (20.0 % → 12.6 % strict), which is the
direction a usable score would show. The intervals, however, overlap completely:
the `score >= 0.70` interval (0.3–36.0 %) contains the
pooled point estimate (20.0 %). At 60 cards the curve cannot
distinguish a real gain in precision from sampling noise.

**No threshold is recommended.** The handoff permits an inconclusive calibration as
a documented result, and that is what this is.

## Limitations

1. **Single adjudicator.** One agent (Opus 5) labelled all 60 cards; there is no second
   adjudicator and therefore no inter-annotator agreement for this deck. The reused H3910
   judge kappa (0.9022) is self-consistency of a different judge on a different population
   and does not transfer.
2. **Evidence is the card, not the source.** Verdicts rest on the glosses carried in the
   table. A truncated gloss can make a correct alignment look wrong and vice versa;
   `C011` and `C014` are explicit instances of contentless glosses.
3. **Band resolution only.** Equal-per-band allocation resolves the curve at the band
   boundaries (0.40, 0.70) and nowhere else; a finer threshold sweep needs a larger deck.
4. **Selection limits.** Cards are drawn only from rows the aligner already aligned and
   only from the gold-excluded remainder, so this measures precision, never recall.
5. **Strata without cards contribute weight but no information** where listed in the JSON.
