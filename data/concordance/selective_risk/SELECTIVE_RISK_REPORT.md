## Error versus coverage

`strict` counts every `different` verdict; `lenient` drops the `near_miss` ones
(metonymy, POS variant, sibling referent); `unsure→wrong` additionally counts every
`unsure` verdict as wrong. These are rubric readings, not bounds on the truth.
Rates are stratified estimates re-weighted to the **6,860 gold-excluded eligible rows**,
not to all 7,087 aligned rows, and never raw deck fractions.
Intervals are a heuristic envelope (wider of a plug-in stratified normal and an unweighted
pooled Wilson interval); they are **not** shown to reach nominal 95 % coverage.

| scope | eligible rows | coverage | cards | strict wrong-match | ~95 % CI | lenient | ~95 % CI | unsure→wrong |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---|---:|
| all eligible (gold-excluded) | 6,860 | 10.20 % | 60 | 18.4 % | 8.8–33.6 % | 10.1 % | 3.0–24.2 % | 21.1 % |
| score >= 0.40 | 5,388 | 8.01 % | 40 | 16.4 % | 5.4–31.9 % | 10.2 % | 1.6–26.1 % | 18.4 % |
| score >= 0.70 | 2,132 | 3.17 % | 20 | 7.4 % | 0.0–30.1 % | 7.4 % | 0.0–30.1 % | 12.6 % |

The point estimate falls monotonically with confidence (18.4 % → 7.4 % strict), which is the
direction a usable score would show. The intervals, however, overlap completely:
the `score >= 0.70` interval (0.0–30.1 %) contains the
pooled point estimate (18.4 %). At 60 cards the curve cannot
distinguish a real gain in precision from sampling noise.

**No threshold is recommended.** The handoff permits an inconclusive calibration as
a documented result, and that is what this is.

## Limitations

1. **Rates rest on adjudicator 1.** Opus 5 labelled all 60 cards; a blind second pass
   (Claude Sonnet 5 (claude-sonnet-5), fresh context, rendered cards only) agrees on 98 % of them,
   Cohen's κ = 0.96 over same/different/unsure (confusion in the JSON). Both are
   agents; neither is the human acceptance vote. The reused H3910 judge kappa (0.9022)
   belongs to a different judge on a different population and does not transfer.
2. **Evidence is the card, not the source.** Verdicts rest on the glosses carried in the
   table. A truncated gloss can make a correct alignment look wrong and vice versa;
   `C011` and `C014` are explicit instances of contentless glosses; both are `unsure`
   (`C014` was relabelled from `different` after the Astra review).
3. **Band resolution only.** Equal-per-band allocation resolves the curve at the band
   boundaries (0.40, 0.70) and nowhere else; a finer threshold sweep needs a larger deck.
4. **Selection limits.** Cards are drawn only from rows the aligner already aligned and
   only from the gold-excluded remainder, so this measures precision, never recall.
5. **Strata without cards contribute weight but no information** where listed in the JSON.
