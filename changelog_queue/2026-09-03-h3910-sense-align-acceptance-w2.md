- **H3910 (Opus 5 `claude-opus-5`) — the acceptance pass for the aligned-sense table: a stratified sample, a two-pass judge that reports its own self-consistency first, and a 120-card human sheet — no precision number yet, because the vote is the verdict.** Two Wave-2 slices shipped with a stated precision gap on purpose ([PR #470](https://github.com/gasyoun/kosha/pull/470), [PR #492](https://github.com/gasyoun/kosha/pull/492)); this closes the measurement half of it and nothing else — **the algorithm is untouched, and `TAU` 0.30 / `GLOSS_FLOOR` 0.20 / `PREFIX_MIN` 4 are not tuned in response to anything measured here.** New [scripts/sample_sense_alignment_acceptance.py](https://github.com/gasyoun/kosha/blob/main/scripts/sample_sense_alignment_acceptance.py) draws **120 of 3,013** aligned rows stratified over `method` × score band × `shape` at a committed `SEED = 3910`, sqrt(N)-proportional with a floor of 3, and records `stratum_population` / `population_share` / `sample_weight` per row so any pooled figure has to be weighted back rather than averaged over strata: `attrib` 31 · `gloss+ls` 39 · `gloss` 20 · `ls` 19 · **`attrib+ls` 9 and `attrib+gloss+ls` 2 enumerated whole**, because at that size a sample of them would measure nothing. The `attrib` channel is oversampled by 30 deliberately — it is one-directional and shipped with a **named** false-positive class, so it is the stratum whose precision is least known. **The judge is a first pass and says so:** one question (*are these the same meaning?*) with a short reason, never a score, a ranking or a repair, run **twice** over re-shuffled and re-numbered cards (`SHUFFLE_SEED = 19103`) — and self-consistency is reported **before any other number**: raw agreement **114/120 = 95.0 %**, **Cohen's κ 0.902**, 94.9 % excluding `unsure`, both passes tallying 65 `different` / 53 `same` / 2 `unsure` ([JUDGE_SELF_CONSISTENCY.md](https://github.com/gasyoun/kosha/blob/main/data/concordance/judge/JUDGE_SELF_CONSISTENCY.md)). The split behind that average is the finding: **`attrib*` strata agree 42/42 = 100 %**, the western-gloss strata 72/78 = 92.3 %, and **all six flips are `gloss+ls` m2m cards** (`Atman#1`, `SiKarin#1`, `dahana#1`, `krUra#2`, `mADava#3`, `vilAsin#1`) — the judge is stable exactly where the alignment is one-directional and unstable exactly where two granularities are being asked to correspond. **The human sheet is the verdict, and nothing on it is pre-marked** — not just in the `attrib` strata where the handoff forbids it, but anywhere, because running two presentation regimes over one measurement would contaminate it; the judge's call and reason are shown as evidence beside the glosses, method, score and witness list. 120 cards, 🔴 on the hub: [kosha_sense_align_acceptance_w2.html](https://gasyoun.github.io/vote/sheets/kosha_sense_align_acceptance_w2.html), built by [scripts/build_sense_alignment_acceptance_sheet.py](https://github.com/gasyoun/kosha/blob/main/scripts/build_sense_alignment_acceptance_sheet.py) through `csl_pyutil.render_review_sheet` (the `review/` output is gitignored here, as all kosha sheets are). **A linter collision worth naming:** `csl_pyutil.evidence.find_slp1`'s D2 marker `[bcdghjklmnprstvy][fxz]` fires on ordinary German orthography — *Anfang*, *Augapfel*, *Blitz*, *Badeplatz* — so the PWG glosses this table exists to show read as SLP1 in human-facing text;
