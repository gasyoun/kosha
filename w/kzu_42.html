kṣubhApte p. 438 ↗
kṣubh 1 A., 4 9 P. (kṣobhate, kṣubhyati, kṣubhnāti, kṣubhita-kṣubdha)
1 To shake, tremble, to be agitated or disturbed; mahāhrada iva kṣubhyan  Bk. 9. 118 ;  R. 4. 21 ;  Śi. 8. 24. 
2 To be unsteady.
3 To stumble (fig. also).
— Caus. (kṣobhayati) To agitate, stir up, excite, perturb.
