kanyāscan ↗
kanyā [ Uṇ. 4. 111 ]
1 An unmarried girl or daughter;  R. 1. 51 , 2. 10 , 3. 33 ;  Ms. 10. 8. 
2 A girl ten years old.
3 A virgin, maiden;  Ms. 8. 367 , 3. 33. 
4 A woman in general.
5 The sixth sign of the zodiac, i. e. Virgo.
6 N. of Durgā.
7 Large cardamoms.
Comp.

— āṭa a. following after or hunting young girls.
— (ṭaḥ)
1 the inner apartments of a house.

— kubjaḥ N. of a
