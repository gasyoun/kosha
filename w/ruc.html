1 To shine, look splendid or beautiful, be resplendent; rurucire rucirekṣaṇavibhramāḥ  Śi. 6. 46 ;  Ms. 3. 62. 
2 To like, be pleased with (said of persons), be agreeable to, please (of things); used with dat. of the person who is pleased and nom. of the thing; na srajā rurucire ramaṇībhyaḥ  Ki. 9. 35 ; yadeva rocate yasmai bhavettattasya suṃdaraṃ  H. 2. 53 ; sometimes with gen. of person; dāridryānmaraṇādvā maraṇaṃ mama rocate na dāridryaṃ  Mk. 1. 11. 
— Caus. (rocayati-te)
1 To cause to like, make pleasant or agreeable;  Ku. 3. 16. 
2 To illuminate, irradiate.
3 To like, find pleasure in
4 To resolve.
— Desid. (ruru-ro-ciṣate) To wish to like &c.
