Monier-Williams

yogakṣemascan ↗
yoga—kṣema m. sg. and pl. (in later language also m. du. and n. sg.) the security or secure possession of what has been acquired, the keeping safe of property, welfare, prosperity, substance, livelihood,  RV.  &c. &c. (generally explained as a Dvaṃdva meaning ‘acquisition and preservation of property’, cf. kṣema-yoga; ˚maṃ-√vah with dat. = to procure any one a livelihood, support, maintain,  Śak. )
yogakṣemascan ↗
the charge for securing property (from accidents), insurance,  Mn. vii, 127 
yogakṣemascan ↗
property destined for pious uses and sacrifices,  Gaut. xxviii, 46 ;  Mn. ix, 219  (others ‘the means of securing protection, i.e. councillors, family priests and the like’)
