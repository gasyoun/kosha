śarīrinscan ↗
śarīrin a. (ṇī f.)
1 Embodied, corporeal, incarnate; karuṇasya mūrtirathavā śarīriṇī birahavyathaiva vanameti jānakī  U. 3. 4 ; bhāvāviva śarīriṇau  M. 1. 10. 
2 Living.
— m.
1 Anything endowed with a body (whether animate or inanimate); śarīriṇāṃ sthāvarajaṃgamānāṃ sukhāya tajjanmadinaṃ babhūva  Ku. 1. 23 ; maraṇaṃ prakṛtiḥ śarīriṇāṃ  R. 8. 87 , 43. 
2 A sentient being.
3 A man.
4 The soul (clad with the body);  R. 8. 89 ;  Bg. 2. 18.
