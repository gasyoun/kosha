Apte 1890

kārmukascan ↗
kārmuka a. (kī f.) [karmaṇe prabhavati ukañ  P. V. 1. 103. ] Fit for or able to do a work, doing it well and completely.
— kaṃ
1 A bow; tatkārmukaṃ karmasu yasya śaktiḥ  Ki. 3. 48 ; tvayi cādhijyakārmuke  Ś. 1. 6. 
2 A bamboo.
3 The ninth sign of the zodiac.
4 A kind of machine or instrument shaped like a bow.
Comp.
— bhṛt m.
1 the archer or the sign Sagittarius of the zodiac.
2 an archer in general.
