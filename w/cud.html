4 To question, ask.

7 To enjoin, lay down;  Ms. 2. 165. 
8 To request, solicit.
9 To help on.
10 To bring or offer quickly.
11 To fix, settle, direct.
12 To be quick.
II. 1 U. Ved. (codati te)
1 To impel, incite.
2 To offer quickly (Soma).
3 To hasten, be quick.
