Apte 1890

bhoginscan ↗
bhogin a. [bhoga-ini]
1 Eating.
2 Enjoying.
3 Suffering, experiencing, enduring.
4 Using, possessing (at the end of comp. in these four senses.)
5 Having curves.
6 Having hoods.
7 Devoted to enjoyment, indulging in sensual pleassures;  Pt. 1. 65  (where it has sense 6 also).
8 Rich, opulent.
— m.
1 A snake; gajājinālaṃbi pinaddhabhogi vā  Ku. 5. 78 ;  R. 2. 32 , 4. 48 , 10. 7 , 11. 59. 
2 A king.
3 A voluptuary.
4 A barber.
5 The headman of a village;
6 The lunar mansion āśleṣā.
— nī A woman belonging to the king's harem, but not consecrated with him, the concubine of a king.
Comp.
— iṃdraḥ, īśaḥ Śeṣa or Vāsuki.
— kāṃtaḥ wind, air.
— bhuj m.
1 an ichneumon.
2 a peacock.
— vallabhaṃ sandal.
