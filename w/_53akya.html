Apte 1890

śakyascan ↗
śakya pot. p.
1 Possible, practicable, capable of being done or effected (usually with an inf.); śa kyo vārayituṃ jalena hutabhuk  Bh. 2. 11 ,  R. 2. 49 , 54. 
2 Fit to be effected.
3 Easy to be effected.
4 Directly conveyed or expressed (as the meaning of a word); śakyo'rtho'bhidhayā jñeyaḥ  S. D. 10. 
5 Potential. (The form śakyaṃ is some times used as a predicative word with an inf. in a passive sense, the real object of the infinitive being in the nom. case; evaṃ hi praṇaya vatī sā śakyamupekṣituṃ kupitā  M. 3. 22 ; śakyaṃ…aviralamāliṃgitu pavanaḥ  Ś. 3. 7 , vibhūtayaḥ śakyamava ptumūrjitāḥ  Subhāṣ. ;  Bg. 18. 11. ).
Comp.
— arthaḥ the meaning directly expressed.
— pratīkāra a. remediable.
