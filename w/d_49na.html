Apte 1890

dīnascan ↗
dīna a. [dī-kta taspa na]
1 Poor, indigent.
2 Distressed, ruined, afflicted, miserable, wretched.
3 Sorry, dejected, melancholy, sad; sā virahe tava dīnā  Gīt. 4. 
4 Timid, frightened.
5 Mean, piteous;  Bh. 2. 51. 
— naḥ A poor person, one in distress or misery; dīnānāṃ kalpavṛkṣaḥ  Mk. 1. 48 ; dināni dīnoddharaṇocitasya  R. 2. 25. 
— naṃ Distress, wretchedness.
— nā The female of a mouse or shrew.
Comp.
— dayālu, -vatsala a. kind to the poor.
— baṃdhuḥ a friend of the poor.
— locanaḥ a cat.
