(p. veviṣat) to consume, eat,  ib.  (cf.  Naigh. ii, 8 );
(p. veviṣāṇa) aided or supported by (instr.),  RV. vii, 18, 15.

viṣscan ↗
viṣ
I. 3 U. (veveṣṭi, veviṣṭe, -viṣṭa)
1 To surround.
2 To spread through, extend, pervade.
3 To embrace.
4 To accomplish, effect, perform.
5 To eat.
6 To go to, go against, encounter; (not generally used in classical literature).
II. 9 P. ( viṣṇāti) To separate, disjoin.
III. 1 P. (veṣati) To sprinkle, pour out.
