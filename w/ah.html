ah
I. 1 A. or 10 U. = aṃh q. v.
II. 1 P. To sing together, compose, celebrate, prepare.
III. 5 P. (ahnoti, āhīt, āha) To pervade.
IV. (a defective verb preserved only in five forms āttha, āhathuḥ, āha, āhatuḥ, āhuḥ)
1 To say, speak, mention.
2 To acknowledge, accept, state.
3 To declare, express, signify.
4 To hold, consider, regard.
5 To call.
