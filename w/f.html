Apte 1890

ṛscan ↗
ṛ ind. An interjection of(1) calling; (2) ridicule; (3) censure or abuse; (4) used at the beginning of a sentence.
ṛscan ↗
ṛ
I. 1 P. (ṛcchati, āra, ārṣīt, ariṣyati, ṛta; caus. arpayati; desid. aririṣati)
1 To go, move; aṃbhaśchāyāmacchāmṛcchati  Śi. 4. 44. 
2 To rise, tend towards.
II. 3 P. (iyarti, ārat, ṛta) (Mostly used in the Veda)
1 To go.
2 To move, shake.
3 To obtain, gain, acquire, reach, meet with.
4 To move, excite, raise (as voice, words &c.) vācamiyarti.
5 To display.
III. 5 P. (ṛṇoti, ṛṇa)
1 To injure, hurt.
2 To attack.
— Caus. (arpayati, ārpipat, arpita)
1 To throw, cast, fling; fix or implant in; hṛdi śalyamarpitaṃ  R. 8. 87. 
2 To put or place on, fix upon, direct or cast towards (as the eye &c.); vāmaprakoṣṭhārpitahemavetraḥ  Ku. 3. 41 ;  Ś. 6. 5 , 17 , 3. 26 ;  R. 17. 79 ;  Ś. 6. 8 ;  Bk. 5. 90 ;  Ku. 6. 11 ;  R. 15. 77 ;  Bg. 8. 7 , 12. 14 ; karapallavārpita  Śi. 9. 54.

5 To give up, sacrifice (as the inherent sense); atra kaliṃgagaṃgāśabdau ātmānamarpayataḥ  S. D. 2. 
6 To give back, restore;  R. 19. 10 ;  Bk. 15. 16 ;  Amaru. 94 ;
