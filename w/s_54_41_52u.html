Apte 1890

sthāṇuApte p. 1148 ↗
sthāṇu a. [sthā-nu pṛṣo˚ ṇatvam] Firm, fixed, steady, stable, immovable, motionless.
— ṇuḥ
1 An epithet of Śiva; sa sthāṇuḥ sthirabhaktiyogasulabho niḥśreyasāyāstu vaḥ  V. 1. 1. 
2 A stake, post, pillar; kiṃ sthāṇurayamuta puruṣaḥ;  Pt. 1. 49. 
3 A peg, pin.
4 The gnomon of a dial.
5 A spear, dart.
6 A nest of white ants.
7 The drug or perfume called Jīvaka.
— m. n. A branchless trunk or stem, any bare stalk or stem, pollard;
Comp.
— chedaḥ one who cuts down the trunks of trees, one who clears away timber; sthāṇucchedasya kedāramāhuḥ śalyavato mṛgaṃ  Ms. 9. 44. 
— bhramaḥ mistaking anything for a post.
