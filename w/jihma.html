jihmascan ↗
jihma a. [jahāti saralamārgaṃ; hā-man sanvat ālopaśca  Uṇ. 1. 138 ]
1
(pb) Sloping, athwart, oblique.
2 Crooked, awry, squint;  Rs. 1. 12. 
3 Tortuous, curved, going irregularly.
4 Curved, bent.
5 Morally crooked, deceitful, dishonest, wicked, unfair; dhṛtahetirapyadhṛtajihmamatiḥ  Ki. 6. 24 ; suhṛdarthamīhitamajihmadhiyāṃ  Śi. 9. 62. 
6 Dim, dark, pale-coloured: vidhisamayaniyogāddīptisaṃhārajihmaṃ  Ki. 1. 46. 
7 Slow, lazy.
— hmaṃ
1 Dishonesty, falsehood.
2 The Tagara tree.

— (gaḥ) a snake.

— yodhin a. fighting unfairly. (
