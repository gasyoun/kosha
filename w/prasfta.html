prasṛtaApte p. 766 ↗
prasṛta p. p.
1 Gone forward.
2 Stretched out, extended.
3 Spread, diffused.
4 Long, lengthened.
5 Engaged in, attached to.
6 Swift, or quick.
7 Manifested, displayed;  U. 6. 14. 
8 Modest, humble.
— taḥ The palm of the hand stretched out and hollowed.
— taḥ,
— taṃ A measure equal to two palas.
— tā The leg.
Comp.
— jaḥ a particular class of sons, an adulterine (kuṃḍagolakarūpa).
