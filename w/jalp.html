jalp (√lap redupl.?) cl. 1. jalpati (ep. also Ā. pf. jajalpa,  R. ) to speak inarticulately, murmur,  ŚBr. xi, 5, 1, 4  ;
to chatter, prattle,  W. ;
to say, speak, converse with (instr. or sārDam),  MBh. ;  R.  &c.;
to speak about (acc.),  MBh. iv, 864 ; v, 4515 ;
= √arc, to praise,  Naigh. iii, 14 ;
(said of the Koïl) to sound (its song),  Bhartṛ.  :
Caus. jalpayati to cause to speak,  Pāṇ. i, 4, 52 , Vārtt. 3.

jalpApte p. 509 ↗
jalp 1 P. (jalpati, jalpita)
1 To speak, talk, speak or converse (with another); aviralitakapolaṃ jalpatorakrameṇa  U. 1. 27 ; ekena jalpaṃtyanalpākṣaraṃ  Pt. 1. 136 ;  Bh. 1. 82. 
2 To murmur, speak inarticulately.
3 To chatter, prattle, babble.
4 To praise.
