dhvanApte p. 603 ↗
dhvan 1 P. (dhvanati, dhvanita) To sound, produce or utter sounds, buzz, hum, echo, reverberate, thunder, roar; bibhidyamānā iva dadhvanurdiśaḥ  Ki. 14. 46 ; ayaṃ dhīraṃ dhīraṃ dhvanati navanīlo jaladharaḥ  Bv. 1. 60 ; kapirdadhvāna meghavat  Bk. 9. 5 ; 14. 3 ; dhvanati madhupasamūhe śravaṇamapidadhāti  Gīt. 5. 
— Caus. (dhvanayati) To cause to sound, ring (as a bell); but dhvānayati ‘to cause to articulate indistinctly’.
