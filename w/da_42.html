dabh, daṃbh I. 1 5 P. (dabhati or dabhnoti, dabdha; desid. dhipsati, dhīpsati, didaṃbhiṣati)
1 To injure, hurt.
2 To deceive, cheat.
3 To go. -II. 10 U. (daṃbhayati-te) To impel, propel, drive onward.
