krūrascan ↗
krūra a. [kṛta-rak dhātoḥ krūḥ cf.  Uṇ. 2. 21 ]
1 Cruel, wicked, hard-hearted, pitiless; tasyābhiṣekasaṃbhāraṃ kalpitaṃ krūraniścayā  R. 12. 4 ;  Me. 105 ;  Ms. 10. 9. 
2 Hard, rough.
3 Formidable, terrible, fierce, ferocious, savage.
4 Destructive, mischievous.
5 Wounded, hurt.
6 Bloody.
7 Raw.
8 Strong.
9 Inauspicious.
10 Hard, solid, hardened;  Ś. 2. 4. 
11 Hot; disagreeable, sharp;  Ms. 2. 33. 
— raḥ, -raṃ Boiled rice.
— raḥ
1 A hawk.
2 A heron.
3 An uneven sign of the zodiac.
4 N. of a planet (Rāhu or Saturn).
— raṃ
1 A wound.
2 Slaughter, cruelty.
3 Any horrible deed.
4 Any frightful appearance.
Comp.
— ākṛti a. terrible in form.
— (tiḥ) epithet of Rāvaṇa.
— ācāra a. following cruel or savage practices.
— āśaya a.
1 containing fierce animals (as a river).
2 of a cruel disposition.
— karman n.
1 a bloody act.
2 any hard labour.
— kṛt a. fierce, cruel, unrelenting.
— koṣṭha a. having costive bowels (unaffected by strong purgatives).
— gaṃdhaḥ sulphur.
— dṛś a.
1 evil-eyed, having a malignant look.
2 mischievous, villainous. (
— m.) N. of Saturn; also of Mars.
