sthira a. [sthā-kirac] (compar. stheyas; superl. stheṣṭha)
1 Firm, steady, fixed; bhāvasthirāṇi jananāṃtarasauhṛdāni  Ś. 5. 2 ; sa sthāṇuḥ sthirabhaktiyogasulabho niḥśreyasāyāstu vaḥ  V. 1. 1 ;  Ku. 1. 30 ;  R. 11. 19. 
2 immovable, still, motionless;  Ku. 2. 38. 
3 Immoveably fixed;  U. 1. 40. 
4 Permanent, eternal, everlasting,  Me. 55 ;  Māl. 10. 25. 
5 Cool, collected, composed, placid, calm.
6 Quiescent, free from passion.
7 Steady in conduct, steadfast.
8 Constant, faithful, determined.
9 Certain, sure.
10 Hard, solid.
11 Strong, intense.
12 Stern, relentless, hard-hearted;  Ku. 5. 47.
