— Caus. To cause to put on. -WITH apa to untie.
— api (api being often changed to pi)
1 to fasten, gird round, bind; atipinaddhena valkalena  Ś. 1 ; maṃdāramālā hariṇā pinaddhā  Ś. 7. 2. 
2 to put on, wear;  Bk. 3. 47. 
3 to cover, envelop; krusumamiva pinaddhaṃ pāṃḍupatrodareṇa  Ś. 1. 19.
