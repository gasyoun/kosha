Apte 1890

tripuraApte p. 1194 ↗
tripura-rī N. of the capital of the Cedis, ‘made noisy by the waves of the Moon's daughter’, i. e. the Narmadā, and, therefore, situated on that river. It is identified with the modern Tevur
6 miles from Jabbalpur.
