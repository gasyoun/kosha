sahasApte p. 1112 ↗
sahas m. [sah -asi]
1 The month called Mārgaśīrṣa;  Śi. 6. 57 ; 16. 47. 
2 The winter season.
— n.
1 Power, might, strength.
2 Force, violence.
3 Victory, conquering.
4 Lustre, brightness.
5 Water.
