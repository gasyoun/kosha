vātaApte p. 958 ↗
vāta p. p. [vā-kta]
1 Blown.
2 Desired or wished for, solicited.
— taḥ
1 Air, wind.
2 The god of wind, the deity presiding over wind.
3 Wind, as one of the three humours of the body.
4 Gout, rheumatism.
5 Inflammation of the joints.
6 A faithless lover (dhṛṣṭa).
Comp.
— aṭaḥ
1 an antelope (vātamṛga)
2 a horse of the sun.
— aṃḍaḥ a disease of the testicles.
— atisāraḥ dysentery caused by some derangement or vitiation of the bodily wind.
— ayaṃ a leaf.
— ayanaḥ a horse.
— (naṃ)
1 a window, an air-hole;  Māl. 2. 11 ;  Ku. 7. 59 ;  R. 6. 24 , 13. 21. 
2 a porch, portico.
3 a pavilion.
