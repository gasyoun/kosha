Apte 1890

miśraApte p. 866 ↗
miśra a. [miśr-ac]
1 Mixed, blended, mingled, combined; gadyaṃ padyaṃ ca miśraṃ ca tat tridhaiva vyavasthitaṃ  Kāv. 1. 11 , 31 , 32 ;  R. 16. 32. 
2 Associated, connected.
3 Manifold, diverse.
4 Tangled, intertwined.
5 (At the end of comp.) Having a mixture of, consisting for the most part of.
6 Mixing, adulterating.
— śraḥ
1 A respectable or worthy person; usually affixed to the names of great men and
(pb) scholars; āryamiśrāḥ pramāṇaṃ  M. 1 ; vasiṣṭhamiśraḥ, maṃḍanamiśraḥ &c.
2 A kind of elephant
3 The group of the constellations kṛttikā and viśākhā.
— śraṃ
1 A mixture.
2 A kind of radish.
Comp.
— jaḥ a mule.
— jāti a. of mixed breed.
— dhānyaṃ mixed grain.
— varṇa a. of a mixed colour.
— (rṇaṃ) a kind of black aloe-wood.
— vyavahāraḥ investigation of composition (of principal and interest).
— śabdaḥ a mule.
