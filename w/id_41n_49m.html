i-dānīm ind. now, at this moment, in this case, just, even (with gen. of ahan, e.g. idānīm ahnaḥ, this present day, ‘nowadays’; idānīm eva, just now; immediately; idānīm api, in this case too; tata idānīm, thereupon, then),  RV. ;  ŚBr. ;  Ait. ;  Ragh.

(in rare cases it is an expletive, affecting but slightly the sense).
