Apte 1890

parasparaApte p. 668 ↗
paraspara a. Mutual; parasparāṃ
(pb) vismayavaṃti lakṣmīmālokayāṃcakrurivādareṇa  Bk. 2. 5. 
— pron. a. Each other, one another (used in the sing. only; often in comp.); parasparasyopari paryacīyata  R. 3. 24 ; 7. 38 ; avijñātaparasparaiḥ apasarpaiḥ 17. 51 ; parasparākṣisādṛkṣyaṃ 1. 40 , 3. 24.  Note. The acc. and abl. singulars are often used adverbially in the sense of ‘mutually,’ ‘reciprocally’, ‘one another,’ ‘by, from, or to one another’, ‘against one another’ &c.; see  Bg. 3. 11 , 10. 9 ;  R. 4. 79 ; 6. 46 ; 7. 14 , 53 ; 12. 94. 
Comp.
— jñaḥ a friend.
