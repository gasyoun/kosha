janman n. [jan bhāve manin]
1 Birth; tāṃ janmane śailavadhūṃ prapede  Ku. 1. 21. 
2 Origin, rise, production, creation; ākare padmarāgāṇāṃ janma kācamaṇeḥ kutaḥ  H. Pr. 44 ;  Ku. 5. 60 ; (at the end
(pb) of comp.) arising or born from; saralaskaṃdhasaṃghadṛjanmā davāgniḥ  Me. 53. 
3 Life, existence; pūrveṣvapi hi janmasu  Ms. 9. 100 ; 5. 38 ;  Bg. 4. 5. 
4 Birth-place.
5 Nativity.
6 A father, giver of birth, progenitor;  Ś. 7. 18. 
7 Natal star.
8 (In astr.) N. of the first mansion or Nakṣatra.
9 A creature, being.
10 People.
11 The people of a household.
12 Kind, race.
13 Nature; property, quality.
14 Custom, manner.
— adhipaḥ

3 regeneration.
4 the other world.
