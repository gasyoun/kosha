barhis m., n. [barh karmaṇi isi]
1 Kuśa grass;  Ku. 1. 60. 
2 A bed or layer of Kuśa grass.
3 A sacrifice, oblation.

2 Light, splendour.

2 Sacrifice.
3 Ether.
