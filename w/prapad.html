prapad 4 A.
1 To enter upon, set forward, set foot in.
2 (a) To go to or towards, approach, resort or attain to, reach; tāṃ janmane śailavadhūṃ prapede  Ku. 1. 21 ; (kṣitīśaṃ) kautsaḥ prapede varataṃtuśiṣyaḥ  R. 5. 1 ;  Bk. 4. 1 ;  Ki. 1. 9 ; 11. 16 ;  R. 8. 11.  (b) To take shelter or refuge with, flee to for safety, submit; śaraṇārthamanyāṃ kathaṃ prapatsye tvayi dīpyamāne  R. 14. 64. 
3 To go or come to a particular state, arrive at, attain to, arrive at or be in a particular condition; reṇuḥ prapede pathi paṃkabhāvaṃ  R. 16. 30 ; muhūrtakarṇotpalatāṃ prapede  Ku. 7. 81 ; bālyātparaṃ sātha vayaḥ prapede  Ku. 1. 31 ; 5. 24 ; īdṛśīmavasthāṃ prapannosmi  Ś. 5 : ṛṣinikarauriti
(pb) saṃśayaḥ prapede  Bv. 4. 33 ;  Amaru. 27. 
4 To get, find, secure, obtain, attain to; partake of share in; sahakāra na prapede madhupena bhavatsamaṃ jagati  Bv. 1. 21 ; kāṃtaṃ vapurvyomacaraṃ prapede  R. 5. 51. 
5 To behave or act towards, deal with; kiṃ prapadyate vaidarbhaḥ  M. 1  ‘what does he propose to do’; paśyāmo mayi kiṃ prapadyate  Amaru. 20. 
6 To admit, allow, agree or consent to;  Y. 2. 40. 
7 To draw near, come on, approach (as time &c.).
8 To be going on, to proceed.
9 To take effect, thrive, prosper.
10 To throw oneself down, fall down (at another's feet).
11 Ved. To attack, assault.
