Apte 1890

bhairavaApte p. 826 ↗
bhairava a. (vī f.) [bhīroridaṃ aṇ]
1 Terrible, frightful, horrible, formidable;  U. 5. 6. 
2 Miserable.
3 Relating to Bhairava.
— vaḥ
1 A form of Śiva (of which
8 kinds are enumerated).
2 The sentiment of terror (bhayānaka).
3 Fear, terror.
4 N. of a musical mode (rāga) calculated to excite emotions of fear or terror.
— vī
1 A form of the goddess Durgā.
2 N. of a Rāgiṇī in the Hindu musical system.
3 A girl of 12 or a young girl representing the goddess Durgā at the Durga festival.
— vaṃ Terror, horror.
Comp.
— īśaḥ an epithet of Viṣṇu (or Śiva ?); so bhairavatarjakaḥ
— yātanā a sort of purificatory torment inflicted by Bhairava of Benares on those who die there, to make their spirits fit for absorption into the Supreme Spirit.
