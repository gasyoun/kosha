Apte 1890

plavaApte p. 781 ↗
plava a. [plu-ac]
1 Swimming, floating.
2 Jumping, leaping.
3 Ved. Superior, excellent.
— vaḥ
1 Swimming, floating.
2 Flood, swelling of a river.
3 A jump, leap.
4 A raft, float, canoe, small boat; nāśayecca śanaiḥ paścātplavaṃ salilapūravat  Pt. 2. 38 ; sarvaṃ jñānaplavenaiva vṛjinaṃ saṃtariṣyasi  Bg. 4. 36 ;  Ms. 4. 194 ; 11. 19 ;  Ve. 3. 25. 
5 A frog.
6 A monkey.
7 A declivity, slope.
8 An enemy.
9 A sheep.
10 A man of a low tribe; a Cāṇḍāla.
11 A net or snare for catching fish.
12 The fig-tree.
13 The Kāraṇḍava bird, a kind of duck.
14 Five or more stanzas syntactically connected. (= kulaka q. v.)
15 The prolated utterance of a vowel.
16 Returning, return.
17 Urging on, inciting.
Comp.
— gaḥ
1 a monkey;  R. 12. 70. 
2
(pb) a frog.
3 an aquatic bird, the diver.
4 the tree śirīṣa.
5 N. of the sun's charioteer.
— (gā) the sign of the zodiac called Virgo.
— gatiḥ a frog.
