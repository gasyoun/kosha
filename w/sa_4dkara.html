saṃkaraMW p. 1126 ↗
saṃ-kara m. mixing together, commingling, intermixture, confusion (esp. of castes or races, proceeding from the intermarriage of a man with a woman of a higher caste or from the promiscuous intercourse of the four tribes, and again from the indiscriminate cohabitation of their descendants; cf. yoni-s˚),  Mn. ;  MBh.  &c.
saṃkaraMW p. 1126 ↗
the offspring of a mixed marriage,  R. 
saṃkaraMW p. 1126 ↗
any action similar to the intermixture of castes (sometimes n.),  MBh. 
saṃkaraMW p. 1126 ↗
