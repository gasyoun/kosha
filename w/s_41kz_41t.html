Apte 1890

sākṣātApte p. 1113 ↗
sākṣāt ind.
1 In the presence of, before the very eyes, visibly, openly, evidently.
2 In person, actually, in bodily form; sākṣāt priyāmupagatāmapahāya pūrvaṃ  Ś. 6. 15 ; 1. 6. 
3 Directly. In comp. it is often translated by ‘incarnate’; sākṣādyamaḥ; or by ‘open, direct’; tatsākṣātpratiṣedhaḥ kopāya  Māl. 1. 11.  ( sākṣātkṛ
1 to see with one's own eyes, realize personally.
2 to have an intuitive perception or manifestation of; sākṣātkṛtadharmāṇa ṝṣayaḥ  U. 7 ).
Comp.
— karaṇaṃ
1 causing to be visibly present,
2 making evident to the senses.
3 intuitive perception.
— kāraḥ perception, apprehension, knowledge.
