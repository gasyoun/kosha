rājanscan ↗
1. rājan m. (ifc. mostly m(-rāja) , esp. in Tat-puruṣas; f(-rājan, ˚jā or ˚jñī). ; cf.  Pāṇ. iv, 1, 28 , Sch.) a king, sovereign, prince, chief (often applied to gods, e.g. to Varuṇa and the other Ādityas, to Indra, Yama &c., but esp. to Soma [also the plant and juice] and the Moon),  RV.  &c. &c.
rājanscan ↗
a man of the royal tribe or the military caste, a Kṣatriya,  ĀśvŚr. ;  ChUp. ;  Mn.  &c. (cf. rājanya)
rājanscan ↗
a Yakṣa,  L. 
rājanscan ↗
N. of one of the 18 attendants on Sūrya (identified with a form of Guha),  L. 
rājanscan ↗
of Yudhiṣṭhira,  MBh.  (rājñām indra-mahotsavaH and rājñām pratibodhaḥ, N. of wks.); ; (rājñī) f. See s.v.
rājanscan ↗
rājan [cf. Lat. rex; Kelt. rîg, fr. which Old Germ. rîk; Goth. reiks; Angl.-Sax. rîce; Eng. rich.]
rājanscan ↗
2. rājan (only in loc. rājani) government, guidance,  RV. x, 49, 4.
