dasyu m. (√das) enemy of the gods (e.g. śambara, śuṣṇa, cumuri, dhuni; all conquered by Indra, Agni, &c.), impious man (called a-śraddha, a-yajña, a-yajyu, a-pṛṇat, a-vrata, anya-vrata, a-karman), barbarian (called a-nās, or an-ās ‘ugly-faced’, adhara, ‘inferior’, a-mānuṣa, ‘inhuman’), robber (called dhanin),  RV. ;  AV.  &c.
