Apte 1890

dvitīyascan ↗
dvitīya a. Second; tvaṃ jīvitaṃ tvamasi me hṛdayaṃ dvitīyaṃ  U. 3. 26 ;  Me. 83 ;  R. 3. 49. 
— yaḥ
1 The second in a family, a son
2 A companion, partner, friend; (usually at the end of comp.); prayataparigrahadvitīyaḥ  R. 1. 95 ;  Ku. 3. 35 ; so chāyā˚, duḥkha˚ &c.
3 The second letter of a class.
4 The second person (in gram.).
— yā
1 The second day of a lunar fortnight.
2 A wife, companion, partner.
3 (In gram.) The accusative case.
— yaṃ The half.
— yaṃ ind. A second time, again.
Comp.
— āśramaḥ the second stage or period of the religious life of a Brāhmaṇa, i. e. gārhasthya.
— vayas a. having arrived at the second stage of life.
