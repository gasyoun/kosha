(pb)
2 Low, soft, sweet (note &c.); melodious, pleasing.
3 Making noise, jingling, tinkling &c.; bhāsvatkalanūpurāṇāṃ  R. 16. 12 ; kalakiṃkiṇīravaṃ  Śi. 9. 74 , 82 ; kalamekhalākalakalaḥ 6. 14 , 4. 57 .
4 Weak.
5 Crude; undigested.
