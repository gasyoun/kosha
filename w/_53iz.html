śiṣ
I. 1 P. (śeṣati) To hurt, kill.
II. 1 P., 10 U., (śeṣati, śeṣayati-te) To leave as a remainder, spare.
III. 7 P. (śinaṣṭi, śiṣṭa)
1 To leave as a remainder, leave, leave remaining.
2 To distinguish or discriminate from others.
— Caus. (śeṣayati-te) To leave &c.
