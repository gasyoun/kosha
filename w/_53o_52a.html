śoṇa a. (ṇā or ṇī f.) [śoṇ-ac]
1 Red, crimson, tinged red; styānāvanaddhaghataśoṇitaśoṇapāṇiruttaṃsayiṣyati kacāṃstava devi bhīmaḥ  Ve. 1. 21 ; āsvāditadvi daśoṇi taśoṇaśobhāṃ  Mu. 1. 8 ;  Ku. 1. 7. 
2 Bay, reddish-brown.
3 Yellow.
— ṇaḥ
1 Crimson, the red colour.
2 Fire
3 A kind of red sugar-cane.
4 A bay horse.
5 N. of a male river, rising in Goṇḍavana and falling into the Ganges near Pātaliputra q. v.; pratyagrahītpārthivavāhinīṃ tāṃ bhāgīrathīṃ śoṇa ivottaraṃgaḥ  R. 7. 36. 
6 The planet Mars; cf. lohita.
— ṇaṃ
1 Blood.
2 Red lead.
Comp.
— aṃbuḥ N. of a cloud which is said to rise at the destruction of the world.
— aśman m.,
— upalaḥ
1 a red stone.
(pb)
2 a ruby.
— padmaṃ a red lotus.
— puṣpakaḥ the Kovidāra tree.
— ratnaṃ a ruby.
