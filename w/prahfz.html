prahṛṣApte p. 769 ↗
prahṛṣ 4 P.
1 To be glad, to rejoice; na prahṛṣyet priyaṃ prāpya  Bg. 5. 20 ; 11. 36. 
2 To stand on end, bristle (as hair of the body).
3 To rejoice before hand, anticipate pleasure.
— Caus. To gladden, exhilarate, delight.
