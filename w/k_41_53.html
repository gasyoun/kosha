kāś 1 4 A. (kāśa-śya-te, kāśita)
1 To shine, look brilliant or beautiful;  R. 10. 86 , 7. 24 ;  Ku. 1. 24 ;  Bk. 2. 25 ;  Śi. 6. 74. 
2 To appear, be visible; naiva bhūmirna ca diśaḥ pradiśo vā cakāśire  Mb. 
3 To appear, or look like.
