I. 5 U. (cinoti, cinute, cikācā-ya, cicye-kye, acaiṣīt-aceṣṭa, cetuṃ, -cita; caus. cāyayati, cāpayati, also cayayati, capayati, desid. cicīṣati, -cikīṣati)
