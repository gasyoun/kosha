2 A brilliant ornament or ornament.
3 An extraordinary appearance, wonder.
4 A sectarial mark on the forehead.
5 Heaven, sky.
6 A spot.
7 The white or spotted leprosy.
8 (In Rhet.) The last of the three main divisions of Kāvya (poetry). It is of two kinds śabdacitra and artha-vācya-citra, and the poetical charm lies mainly in the use of figures of speech dependent on the sound or sense of words. Mammaṭa thus defines it: -śabdacitraṃ vācyacitramavyaṃgyaṃ tvavaraṃ smṛtaṃ  K. P. 1.  As an instance of śabdacitra may be cited the following verse from  R. 9 Anything bright which strikes the eye.
10 Playing upon words, punning, using conundrums, riddles &c.
