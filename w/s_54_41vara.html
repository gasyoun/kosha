Apte 1890

sthāvaraApte p. 1149 ↗
sthāvara a. [sthā-varac]
1 Fixed to one spot, stable, stationary, immovable, inanimate (opp. jaṃgama); śarīriṇāṃ sthāvarajaṃgamānāṃ sukhāya tajjanmadinaṃ babhūva  Ku. 1. 23 , 6. 67 , 73. 
2 Inert, inactive, slow.
3 Regular, established.
— raḥ A mountain; sthāvarāṇāṃ himālayaḥ  Bg. 10. 25. 
— raṃ
1 Any stationary or inanimate object (such as clay, stones, trees &c. which formed the seventh creation of Brahman; cf.  Ms. 1. 41 ); mānyaḥ sa me sthāvarajaṃgamānāṃ sargasthitipratyavahārahetuḥ  R. 2. 44 ;  Ku. 6. 58. 
2 A bow-string.
3 immovable property, real estate.
4 A heir-loom.
Comp.
— asthāvaraṃ, -jaṃgamaṃ
1 moveable and immovable property.
2 animate
(pb) and inanimate things.
— ātman a. of immovable form;  Ku. 6. 67.
