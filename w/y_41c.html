yācMW p. 850 ↗
yāc cl. 1. P. Ā. ( Dhātup. xxi, 3 ) yācati, ˚te (usually Ā. in sense of ‘asking for one's self’; pf. yayāca Gr., yayāce,  Br.  &c.; aor. ayācīt, ˚ciṣṭa Subj. yāciṣat, ˚ṣāmahe,  RV. ; Prec. yācyāt Gr.; fut. yācitā,  ib. ; yāciṣyati, ˚te,  Br.  &c.; inf. yācitum,  AV.  &c.; ind.p. yācitvā, -yācya,  Br.  &c.),
to ask, beg, solicit, entreat, require, implore (with double acc.; or with abl., rarely gen. of pers.; the thing asked may also be in acc. with prati, or in dat., or ibc. with arthe, or artham),  RV.  &c. &c.;
(with punar) to ask anything back,  TBr. ;
(with kanyām) to be a suitor for a girl, to ask a girl in marriage from (abl., rarely acc.) or for (kṛte or arthe; also with vivāhārTam),  MBh. ;  Kāv.  &c.;
to offer or tender anything (acc.) to (dat.),  AV. ;
to promise (?),  ib.  :
Pass. yācyate, to be asked (‘for’, acc.; rarely of things),  MBh. ;  Kāv.  &c.;
Caus. yācayati (˚te,  AV. ; aor. ayayācat,  Pāṇ. vii, 4, 2 ),
to cause to ask or woo,  MBh. ;
to request anything (acc.) for (arthe),  Pañcat.  :
Desid. yiyāciṣate,  Pāṇ. vi, 1, 8 , Vārtt. 3,  Pat.  :
Intens. yāyācyate, yāyākti Gr.
