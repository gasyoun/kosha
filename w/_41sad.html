1 (a) To meet with, find; mṛtyubhayamāsāditaḥ  Bhāg.  (b) To get, obtain; amaragaṇatālekhyamāsādya  R. 8. 95 ;  Ms. 4. 227 ; dhanaṃ, sukhaṃ &c.
