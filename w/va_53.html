vaś cl. 2. P. ( Dhātup. xxiv, 71 ) vaṣṭi, (1. pl. uśmasi, or śmasi,  RV. ; 3. pl. uśanti,  ib. ; p. uśat, uśāna and uśamāna,  ib. ; cl. 1. vaśati and cl. 3. vivaṣṭi [also vavaṣṭi], 2. sg. vavakṣi,  ib. ; pf. vāvaśuḥ, ˚śe; p. ˚śāna,  ib. ; uvāśa, ūśuḥ Gr.; aor. avāśīt or avaśīt,  ib. ; 2. sg. vaśīh,  MBh.  Prec. uśyāt Gr.; fut. vaśitā, ˚śiṣyati,  ib. ),
to will, command (p. uśamāna, ‘having at command’),  RV. ;  AV. ;
to desire, wish, long for, be fond of, like (also with inf.),  RV.  &c. &c. (p. uśat and uśāna, ‘willing, glad, eager, zealous, obedient’; the former with superl. uśat-tama, also = charming, lovely,  BhP.  [accord. to Sch. also = śuddha, dedīpyamāna, and sv-arcita]);
to aver, maintain, affirm, declare for (two acc.),  MaitrUp. ;  VarBṛS. ;  BhP.  :
Caus. vāśayati (aor. avīvaśat), to cause to desire &c.;
to get in one's power, subject,  Cat.  :
Desid. vivaśiṣati, Gr.;
Intens. vāvaśyate, vāvaśīti, or vāvaṣṭi,  ib. ;
p. vāvaśāna, ‘willing, eager’,  RV. [cf. Gk. ἑκών, for ϝεκων, ‘willing’.]
