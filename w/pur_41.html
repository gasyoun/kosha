purāscan ↗
purā ind.
1 In former times, formerly, of yore, in the olden time; purā śakramupasthāya  R. 1. 75 ; purā sarasi mānase… yasya yātaṃ vayaḥ  Bv. 1. 3 ;  Ms. 1. 119 ; 5. 22. 
2 Before, hitherto, upto the present time.
3 At first, in the first place.
4 In a short time, soon, ere-long, shortly (in this sense usually with a present tense to which it gives a future sense); purā saptadvīpāṃ jayati vasudhāmapratirathaḥ  Ś. 7. 33 ; purā dūpayati sthalīṃ 12. 30 ; āloke te nipatati purā sā balivyāṃkulāṃ vā  Me. 85 ;  N. 1. 18 ;
(pb)  Śi. 10. 56 ;  Ki. 10. 50 ; 11. 36. 
5 Ved. For the defence of
6 Securely from.
7 Except, besides.
