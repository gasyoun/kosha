pṛśniApte p. 719 ↗
pṛśni(ṣṇi) a. [spṛa-ni˚ kicca pṛṣo˚ salopaḥ;  Uṇ. 4. 52 ]
1 Short, small, dwarfish.
2 Delicate, feeble.
3 Diversified, spotted.
4 Various, diverse (Ved.).
— śniḥ A dwarf.
— śni f.
1 A ray of light.
2 The earth.
3 The starry sky.
4 N. of Devakī, mother of Kṛṣṇa.
5 The earth.
6 A cloud.
7 Milk.
Comp.
— garbhaḥ, -dharaḥ, -bhadraḥ epithets of Kṛṣṇa.
— śṛṃgaḥ
1 an epithet of Kṛ ṣṇa.
2 of Gaṇeśa.
