ukṣ 1 6 U. (ukṣati, ukṣāṃcakāra, vavakṣa-kṣe Ved., aukṣīt, ukṣituṃ, ukṣita)
1 To sprinkle, wet, moisten, pour down upon; aukṣan śoṇitamaṃbhodāḥ  Bk. 17. 9 , 3. 5 ;  Śi. 5. 30 ;  R. 11. 5 ,
(pb) 20 ;  Ku. 1. 54. 
2 To emit, send forth.
3 To scatter, throw out (as sparks).
4 To clean, purify.
5 To grow up, become strong (Ved.).
