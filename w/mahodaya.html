Apte 1890

mahodayascan ↗
mahodaya (also called kānyakubja or gādhinagara) is the same as the modern Kānyakubja or Kanoja, on the Ganges. In the seventh century it was the most celebrated place in India. Cf.  B. R. 10. 88-89 .
