1. lap (cf. √rap) cl. 1. P. ( Dhātup. xi, 8 ) lapati (ep. also ˚te and lapyati; pf. lalāpa, lepus,  MBh.  &c.; aor. alāpīt Gr.; fut. lapitā,  ib. ; lapiṣyati,  Br.  &c.; inf. laptum,  MBh. ; lapitum,  Kāv. ; ind.p. -lapya,  MBh. ),
to prate, chatter, talk (also of birds),  MBh. ;  Hariv. ;
to whisper,  Gīt. ;  Pañcar. ;
to wail, lament, weep,  Nalod.  :

lālapyate (mc. also ˚ti), to wail, lament,  MBh. ;  R. ;  MārkP. ;
to address repeatedly,  MBh. , lālapti Gr.
