beginning, commencement,  ŚBr.  &c. &c. (ifc. = ‘commencing with’ or ‘et caeteraet cætera’ e.g. munayaḥ somaśravaḥ-prabhṛtayaḥ, ‘the Munis beginning with S˚’ i.e. ‘the Munis, S˚ &c.’; in this sense also ˚tika)
prabhṛtiscan ↗
pra-˚bhṛti ind. (after an abl. adv. or ifc.) beginning with, from—forward or upward, since,  GṛŚrS. ;  Mn. ;  MBh.  &c. (e.g. bālyāt prabhṛti, ‘from boyhood upwards’; janma-pr˚, ‘from birth’; adya pr˚, ‘beginning from today, henceforth’; tataḥ or tadā pr˚, ‘thenceforth’ &c.)
