1 Falsely, wrongly, untruly, lyingly; yadvaktraṃ muhurīkṣase na dhanināṃ brūṣe na cāṭuṃ mṛṣā  Bh. 3. 147 ; mṛṣābhāṣāsiṃdho  Bv. 2. 21. 
2 In vain, to no purpose, uselessly.

— jñānaṃ ignorance, error.
