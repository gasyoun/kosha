mṛṣāApte p. 877 ↗
mṛṣā ind.
1 Falsely, wrongly, untruly, lyingly; yadvaktraṃ muhurīkṣase na dhanināṃ brūṣe na cāṭuṃ mṛṣā  Bh. 3. 147 ; mṛṣābhāṣāsiṃdho  Bv. 2. 21. 
2 In vain, to no purpose, uselessly.
Comp.
— adhyāyin m. a kind of crane.
— arthaka a.
1 untrue.
2 absurd.
— (kaṃ) an absurdity, an impossibility.
— udyaṃ falsehood, lying, a false statement; tatkiṃ manyase rājaputri mṛṣodyaṃ taditi  U. 4. 
— jñānaṃ ignorance, error.
— bhāṣin, -vādin m. a liar.
— vāc f. an untrue or satirical speech, satire, irony.
— vādaḥ
1 an untrue speech; a lie, falsehood.
2 insincere speech, flattery.
3 irony, satire.
