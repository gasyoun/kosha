Apte 1890

dhāraṇaApte p. 596 ↗
dhāraṇa a. (ṇī f.) [dhṛ-ṇic-lyuṭ] Holding, bearing, carrying, preserving, sustaining, protecting, having, assuming, &c.
— ṇau (du.) The two female breasts.
— ṇaṃ
1 The act
(pb) of holding, bearing, supporting, preserving or keeping back.
2 Possessing, possession.
3 Observing; holding fast.
4 Retaining in the memory; grahaṇadhāraṇapaṭurbālakaḥ.
5 Being indebted (to any one.)
6 Steady abstraction of the mind.
7 Keeping, maintaining.
8 Restraining.
9 (In gram.) Pronouncing imperfectly,
— ṇī
1 A row or line.
2 A vein or tubular vessel.
3 Steadiness.
