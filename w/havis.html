Apte 1890

havisApte p. 1168 ↗
havis n. [hūyate hu-karmaṇi asun]
1 An oblation or burnt offering in general; vahati vidhihutaṃ yā haviḥ  Ś. 1. 1 ;  Ms. 3. 87 , 132 ; 5. 7 , 6. 12. 
2 Clarified butter.
3 Water.
4 N. of Śiva.
5 A sacrifice.
Comp.
— aśanaṃ (haviraśanaṃ) devouring clarified butter or oblations.
— (naḥ) fire.
— gaṃdhā (haviṃrgadhā) the Śamī tree.
— gehaṃ (havirgehaṃ) a house in which sacrificial oblations are offered.
— bhuj m. (havirbhuj) fire; anvāsitamaruṃdhatyā svāhayeva havirbhujaṃ  R. 1. 56 , 10. 69 , 13. 41 ;  Ku. 5. 20 ,  Śi. 1. 2 ;  Kāv. 2. 168. 
— yajñaḥ ( haviryajñaḥ) a kind of sacrifice.
— yājin (haviryājin) m. a priest.
