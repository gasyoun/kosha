liṅgascan ↗

a proof, evidence,  Kaṇ. ;  KātyŚr. ;  Sarvad.

the sign of gender or sex, organ of generation,  Mn. ;  Hariv. ;  Pur.  &c.
liṅgascan ↗
the male organ or Phallus (esp. that of Śiva worshipped in the form of a stone or marble column which generally rises out of a yoni, q.v., and is set up in temples dedicated to Śiva; formerly 12 principal Śiva-liṅgas existed, of which the best known are Soma-nātha in Gujarāt, Mahā-kāla at Ujjayinī, Viśveśvara at Benares &c.; but the number of Liṅgas in India is estimated at 30 millions,  IW. 322 n. ;  RTL. 78, 1 ; 90 ),  MBh. ;  R.  &c.
liṅgascan ↗
gender (in gram.; cf. puṃ-l˚),  Prāt. ;  Pāṇ. 
liṅgascan ↗
the image of a god, an idol,  VarBṛS. 
liṅgascan ↗
(in logic) = vyāpya, the invariable mark which proves the existence of anything in an object (as in the proposition ‘there is fire because there is smoke’, smoke is the liṅga; cf.  IW. 62 )

inference, conclusion, reason (cf. kāvya-l˚)

liṅgascan ↗
(in Sāṃkhya) = prakṛti or pradhāna, ‘the eternal procreative germ’,  L. 
liṅgascan ↗
= vyakta,  L. 
liṅgascan ↗
cf.  RTL. 30 
liṅgascan ↗
= prātipadika, the crude base or uninflected stem of a noun (shortened into li),  Vop. , Sch.
liṅgascan ↗
(in rhet.) an indication (word that serves to fix the meaning of another word; e.g. in the passage kupito makara-dhvajaḥ the word kupita restricts the meaning of makara-dhvaja to ‘Kāma’) = liṅga-purāṇa,  BhP. 
liṅgascan ↗
the order of the religious student,  W. 
liṅgascan ↗
a symptom, mark of disease,  W.
