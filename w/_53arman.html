Apte 1890

śarmanApte p. 1036 ↗
śarman a. [śṝ-manin  Uṇ. 4. 144 ] Happy, prosperous.
— m.
1 An affix added to the name of a Brāhmaṇa; as viṣṇuśarman; cf. varman, dāsa, gṛpta.
— n.
1 Pleasure, happiness, delight; tyajaṃtyasūn śarma ca mānino varaṃ tyajāṃti na tvekamayācitaṃ vrataṃ  N. 1. 50 ;  R. 1. 69 ;  Bh. 3. 97. 
2 A blessing.
3 Protection.
4 A house, receptacle (mostly Vedic in this sense).
Comp.
— da a. conferring happiness.
— (daḥ) an epithet of Viṣṇu.
