sadā ind. Always, ever, perpetually, at all times.
Comp.
— ānaṃda a. ever happy.
— (daḥ) an epithet of Śiva.
— gati
1 wind.
2 the
(pb) universal spirit.
3 the sun.
4 everlasting happiness, final beatitude.
— toyā, -nīrā
1 N. of the Karatoyā river.
2 a river always bearing water, a running stream.
— dāna a. always making gifts or exuding rut; sadādānaḥ parikṣīṇaḥ śasta eva karīśvaraḥ  Pt. 2. 70. 
— (naḥ)
1 a ruttish elephant.
2 a scent-elephant (= gaṃdhadviṣa q. v.).
3 N. of the elephant of Indra.
4 N. of Gaṇeśa.
— nartaḥ a kind of bird, the wagtail
— puṣpa a. everflowering.
— (ṣpaḥ) the cocoa-nut tree.
— prasūnaḥ N. of various plants:- sohita, arka and kuṃda.
— phala a. always bearing fruit.
— (laḥ)
1 the Bilva tree.
