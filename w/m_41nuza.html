mānuṣascan ↗
mānuṣa a. (ṣī f.) [manorayaṃ aṇ suk ca]
1 Human; mānuṣī tanuḥ, mānuṣī vāk &c.;  R. 1. 60 , 16. 22 ;  Bg. 4. 12 ; 9. 11 ;  Ms. 4. 124. 
2 Humane, kind.
— ṣaḥ
1 A man, human being.
2 An epithet of the three signs of the zodiac, Gemini, Virgo, and Libra.
— ṣī
1 A woman.
2 A branch of medicine, administering drugs and herbs.
— ṣaṃ
1 Humanity.
2 Human effort or action.
