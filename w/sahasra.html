sahasraMW p. 1195 ↗
sahasra n. (rarely) m. (perhaps fr. 7. sa + hasra = Gk. χίλιοι for χεσλοι; cf. Pers. hazār) a thousand (with the counted object in the same case sg. or pl., e.g. sahasreṇa bāhunā, ‘with a thousand arms’,  Hariv. ; sahasraṃ bhiṣajaḥ, ‘a thousand drugs’,  RV. ; or in the gen., e.g. dve sahasre suvarṇasya, ‘two th˚ pieces of gold’,  Rājat. ; catvāri sahasrāṇi varṣāṇām, ‘four th˚ years’,  Mn. ; sometimes in comp., either ibc., e.g. yuga-sahasram, ‘a th˚ ages’,  Mn. , or ifc., e.g. sahasrāSvena, ‘with a th˚ horses’; sahasram may also be used as an ind., e.g. sahasram ṛṣibhiḥ, ‘with a th˚ Ṛṣis’,  RV. ; with other numerals it is used thus, ekāDikaM sahasram, or eka-sahasram, ‘a th˚ one’, 1001; dvyadhikaṃ s˚, ‘a th˚ two’, 1002; ekādaśāDikam s˚sahasram or ekādaśaṃ s˚ or ekādaśa-s˚, ‘a th˚ eleven’ or ‘a th˚ having eleven’, 1011; viṃśaty-adhikaṃ s˚ or vimaṃ s˚, ‘a th˚ twenty’, 1020; dve sahasre or dvi-sahacram, ‘two th˚’; trīṇi sahasrāṇi or tri-sahasram, ‘three th˚’ &c.),  RV.  &c. &c.
sahasraMW p. 1195 ↗
a thousand cows or gifts (= sahasraṃ gavyam &c., used to express wealth; sahasraṃ śatāSvam, ‘a th˚ cows and a hundred horses’,  ŚāṅkhŚr. ),  RV. ;  VS. ;  ŚBr.  (in later language often = ‘1000 Paṇas’, e.g.  Mn. viii, 120 ; 336  &c.)
sahasraMW p. 1195 ↗
any very large number (in  Naigh. iii, 1  among the bahu-nāmāni; cf. sahasra-kiraṇa &c. below)
sahasraMW p. 1195 ↗
sahasra mf(ī)n. a thousandth or the thousandth (= sahasra-tama which is the better form; cf.  Pāṇ. v, 2, 57 ).
