spandMW p. 1268 ↗
spand (often confounded with √syand) cl. 1. Ā. ( Dhātup. ii, 13 ) spandate (rarely ˚ti; only in pres. base and inf. spanditum; Gr. also pf. paspande; fut. spanditā, spandiṣyate; aor. aspandiṣṭa),
to quiver, throb, twitch, tremble, vibrate, quake, palpitate, throb with life, quicken (as a child in the womb),  PārGṛ. ;  Car. ;  MBh.  &c.;
to kick (as an animal),  Br. ;  ĀśvŚr. ;
to make any quick movement, move, be active,  Hariv. ;
to flash into life, come suddenly to life,  BhP.  :
Caus. spandayati (aor. apaspandat), to cause to quiver or shake,  MBh. ;
to move (trans.),  ĀśvŚr.  :
Desid. pispandiṣate Gr.:
Intens., See paniṣpada.
