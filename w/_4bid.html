khid
I. 6 P. (khiṃdati, khinna) To strike, press down, afflict.
II. 4 7 A. (khidyate, khiṃtte, khinna) To suffer pain or misery, to be afflicted or wearied, feel tired, depressed or exhausted;  Ś. 5. 7 ; sa puruṣo yaḥ khidyate neṃdriyaiḥ  H. 2. 141  overpowered; kiṃ nāma mayi khidyate garu  Ve. 1 ;  Śānti. 3. 7 ;  Bk. 14. 108 , 17. 10. 
— Caus.
1 To frighten, terrify.
2 To exhaust, fatigue, make tired.
