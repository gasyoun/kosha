gras
I. 1 A. [grasate, grasta]
1 To swallow, devour, eat up, consume;
(pb) sa imāṃ pṛthivīṃ kṛtsnāṃ saṃkṣipya grasate punaḥ  Mb. ;  Bg. 11. 30. 
2 To seize.
3 To eclipse; dvāveba grasate dineśvaraniśāprāṇeśvarau bhāsurau  Bh. 2. 34 ; himāṃśumāśu grasate tanmradimnaḥ sphuṭaṃ phalaṃ  Śi. 2. 49. 
4 To slur over words.
5 To destory. -WITH saṃ to destroy;  Bk. 12. 4. 
II. 1 P., 10 U. (grasati, grāsayati-te) To eat, devour.
